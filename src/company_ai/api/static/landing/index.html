04 / Privacy

Why private

If the useful context is sensitive, the data boundary is not a detail.

  • Company context needs control: policies, contracts, meeting notes, customer records, and internal decisions should not be pasted into generic tools without clear boundaries.
  • Private deployment is an architecture choice: run in a private cloud, EU-controlled environment, or on-prem setup when the workflow requires it.
  • Not every step needs a frontier model: routine extraction, classification, routing, and structured summaries can often use smaller controlled models at lower cost.
  • Audit posture matters: every generated answer is designed to carry citations back to the notes, emails, documents, records, or memos it used.
  • You own the system: database, artifacts, prompts, deployment, and model-routing decisions stay under your control.

08 / FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask

Private AI is not just a model choice. It is a workflow and data-boundary decision.

Is this just another chatbot?

No. The demo is built around specific knowledge workflows: topic catch-ups, decision archaeology, client briefings, risk checks, and follow-ups. The assistant answers from retrieved source material and shows where the answer came from.

Why not just use Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, or another SaaS AI tool?

Use them when the data boundary is acceptable. This architecture is for workflows where sensitive company context needs explicit control over where prompts go, what gets stored, who can access logs, and which model sees the data.

Does this replace our existing tools?

No. It sits above the tools where context already lives: email, documents, notes, tickets, project systems, knowledge bases, and, where relevant, CRM exports. The point is to make scattered context usable without forcing a new source of truth.

What would a real deployment add?

The public demo uses synthetic data. A real deployment adds your connectors, permissions, privacy rules, evaluation set, monitoring, and workflow-specific review steps.
