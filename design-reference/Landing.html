01 / Capability

Your company memory, queryable in plain language.

Account teams already have the information — it sits in inboxes, transcripts, CRM notes, and the PDF your champion sent in November. Panea Memory indexes that material inside your own boundary and gives the people who need it a single, source-backed answer.

01

Ingests what you already have

Emails, meeting transcripts, CRM exports, contracts, decks, scanned PDFs. OCR'd material is flagged so reviewers know the difference between extracted and original text.

02

Answers with citations

Every assistant response points to the underlying artifact — email message ID, transcript turn, PDF page. No claim without a source you can click and verify.

03

Flags what changed

Surfacing risks, stalled threads, and shifts in tone is the point. Alerts are evidence-linked, severity-tagged, and dismissible — not noise.

02 / Privacy model

Your accounts never leave the boundary you already control.

The system is built around a clear data line. Inference runs on infrastructure you own or rent in a jurisdiction you choose. Open-weight models — Llama, Mistral, Qwen — are the default. Vendor-hosted APIs are opt-in per workflow, never the path of least resistance.

04 / FAQ

Anticipated objections.

Is this another RAG wrapper?

Retrieval is the floor, not the product. The product is the workflow around it — citation discipline, alerting on what changed, evaluation as a first-class artifact, and the operational shape that lets a regulated business actually deploy it. The retrieval substrate is replaceable.

Why open weights, not Claude or GPT?

Open weights are the default because they make the data-flow story explainable to a CISO in one diagram. Hosted models are not banned — they're an opt-in per workflow. Some teams will run Claude for drafting and Llama for retrieval grounding; that's a configuration choice, not an architecture one.

What about hallucinations?

Two structural controls: every assistant response must cite a retrieved artifact, and the eval set tracks citation precision over time. A model that paraphrases without grounding fails the eval gate before it ships. The demo enforces the same rule — every cited claim is clickable.

Can our partner consultancy deploy this?

Yes. The reference deployment is documented for partner consultancies — Salesforce / HubSpot / RevOps shops, GDPR consultancies. The pilot engagement includes a handoff package: runbook, eval set, audit log schema, and a written model-of-the-system the partner can take to a maintenance contract.

What does the demo actually show?

Three synthetic accounts at different deal stages, each with realistic-shaped artifacts: emails, meeting transcripts, an OCR'd security policy excerpt, a CRM-style note, and an internal doc. The conversation, alerts, and citations are live against those accounts. The model running the public demo is rate-limited; private walkthroughs use the production configuration.
